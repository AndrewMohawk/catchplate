# Current LIVE grants over historical activation and consume evidence

The `.228` source connection uses the existing original-only grant producer,
issuer worker and independent delivery receiver. It adds no grant schema,
descriptor, incoming success carrier or alternate durable slot. Source only:
unbuilt, unexecuted and unprovisioned.

## Three original authorities

The genuinely admitted proof bootstrap v2 carries the current R2 activation
planner template and original completion custody v2/history v11. The latter
independently authenticates R1 consume evidence and R0 activation provenance.
The grant producer does not accept those materials from a caller: it derives
them by verifying the independently held policy and exact proof original.

R1 `completion_material.evidence_root_public_key` verifies the original state,
caller and grant enrollments and signed COMPLETE/ACK. Current R2 verifies the
release custody, intent and proof and signs the new storage authorization,
LIVE server bootstrap and client authorization. The current custody planner
must equal the exact R2 template original from the proof. Genuine R1 and R2
state-enrollment payloads, database and generation must agree, though their
signed enrollment bytes may differ. The delivery-key digest must equal the
genuinely authenticated R0 history's delivery key. There is no state migration,
AES-key substitution or renewal of an original delivery/control expiry.

The independent grant-delivery receiver repeats original policy/proof/server
admission, the current-template byte join and the full state/delivery-key
compatibility checks. It does not trust a producer-returned material. Legacy
proof v1 retains its single-root and exact state-original requirements.

## Actual bounded worker path

The source-fixed raw8 grant request still wraps LIVE raw7; the producer returns
only raw2 client/server originals. The six inherited issuer descriptors and
one-use socket/staging ownership are unchanged. The dynamic proof-input policy
reader permits at most 6 MiB; the genuine verifier still caps v1 at 64 KiB.
Enclosing limits may be smaller.

Before nested proof decode, `_proof_reservation(policy_size, proof_size)`
reserves sixteen actual proof footprints, eight actual policy footprints and
128 KiB of fixed metadata. The delivery receiver also reserves eight actual
server-original footprints. Issuer startup checks FD lengths before body one
and reserves its held cold graph plus the producer's subsequent re-admission,
alongside prior dynamic-owner originals and the bounded request/response lane.
The dynamic receiver tightens the fresh-proof body cap before intake and
counts fixed known R0/R1 history, bridge-policy and R2 planner fields after
genuine admission. Its policy capture reservation includes the separately
decoded v2 planner. Every stage retains the original work, clocks and 64 MiB
aggregate; these representation limits are not a general heap/RSS proof.

## Remaining boundary

LIVE/checkpoint zero is independently signed authority, never inferred from
missing records. The actual private release owner must retain and read back
the exact token-free outcome before opening the pinned envelope. Staging ACK
is not worker readiness or delivery. Existing durable issuer/dispatch claims,
normal cleanup, fresh current-auth and final signed cookie checks remain
mandatory outside this producer.

This does not migrate a retired intent/proof/custody signer or a retained
outcome to another root. A newly signed intent changes evidence identity and
cannot stand in for an existing outcome. RECOVERY/checkpoint one requires its
own independent policy and exact original-store readback; no automatic retry,
checkpoint switch or SQL retry follows from this LIVE connection. Historical
release-authority migration, explicit older-source adapters and external
production acceptance remain separate work.
